Step 3

Paste it into your email app

  1. In Gmail on a computer, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "DBR".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V or ⌘+V). The logo should appear.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose "DBR" for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Send yourself a test email.

This signature also appears when you use the Gmail app on your phone with a Google account, so there's nothing else to set up there.

  1. Open Mail. In the menu bar choose Mail → Settings (called Preferences on older macOS), then the Signatures tab.
  2. Select your DBR account in the left column and click +. Name the new signature "DBR".
  3. Untick "Always match my default message font" under the middle column. If this stays ticked, Mail strips the brand fonts and colours.
  4. Delete the placeholder text in the right-hand box and paste (⌘+V).
  5. At the bottom, set Choose Signature to "DBR". Close the window and it saves automatically.
  6. Send a test email to a Gmail or Outlook address and check that the logo shows inline, not as an attachment.

If the logo arrives as an attachment, delete the signature, copy again from this page using Safari, and paste it again.

  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad, fill in your details and tap Copy signature.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature. On older iOS it is Settings → Mail → Signature.
  3. Choose Per Account if you have more than one, clear the DBR box and paste.
  4. If it pastes as plain text, shake the phone straight away and tap Undo Change Attributes. The formatting comes back.

iPhone Mail sometimes shrinks or simplifies signatures. Check one sent email; if it looks wrong, use the Gmail app for work email on your phone instead.
